    What a piece really costs

    Jewellers bill the 22K metal at today's rate, add a making charge (value addition, usually 8–25%), sometimes a wastage charge, then 3% GST on the total. Hallmarked 916 jewellery exchanges back at the metal value only — the making charge is not recovered. The gallery below prices typical pieces with your settings.

      How the price is built

      1. International spot — gold in USD per troy ounce (live: gold-api.com; history: COMEX GC=F via Yahoo Finance).
      2. Convert — ÷ 31.1035 g per oz × USD/INR (ECB reference rate via Frankfurter).
      3. Land it in India — add customs duty (6% since 23 Jul 2024; 15% before that; 10.75% before Jul 2022) — the schedule is applied date-by-date across the history.
      4. Calibrate — scale to the latest IBJA 999 rate so the level matches what Indian bullion actually trades at (factor shown below).
      5. Purity — 22K = 91.6%, 18K = 75%. Jewellers add making charges (VA) and 3% GST on top.

      The buy signal

      Six standard technical checks are scored out of 100: RSI-14, distance from the 50- and 200-day averages, drawdown from the 52-week high, position inside the Bollinger band, and 20-day momentum. 70+ is a strong dip, 55–69 accumulate, 40–54 neutral (keep a monthly SIP), below 40 wait. The forecast band is a log-normal projection using the long-run drift and one year of daily volatility — it is a range of likely outcomes, not a prediction of a single price.
